Plants make a tank look better and help it stay healthy. They take up nitrate, they compete with algae for light and nutrients, and they give fish somewhere to hide.

## Hardy starter plants

- **Anubias:** tough leaves, slow growing, copes with low light.
- **Java fern:** similar needs, and easy to propagate.
- **Java moss:** fills gaps and gives shrimp and fry cover.

## How to plant them

These three don’t want to be buried. Tie or glue them to wood or stone. If the thick stem (the rhizome) is under the substrate, the plant can rot.

## What they need

Moderate light, regular water changes, and patience. Slow growth is normal. If you want faster growth later, you can learn about fertiliser and CO2, but you don’t need either to start.
