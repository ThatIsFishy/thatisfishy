Cloudy water is one of the most common beginner worries. The colour tells you a lot.

## White or milky

Usually a bacterial bloom in a new tank. It looks alarming but it is normally harmless and clears by itself in a few days. Don’t clean the filter, and avoid overfeeding. Test ammonia to be safe.

## Green

Algae floating in the water, often from too much light, or from too much light plus nutrients. Shorten the lighting period, keep direct sunlight off the tank, and keep up with water changes.

## Yellow or brown

Often tannins from driftwood. It is harmless and some fish like it. Pre-soaking wood reduces it, and activated carbon in the filter removes it.

## What to do first, every time

1. Test ammonia and nitrite.
2. Check you haven’t overfed.
3. Do a partial water change with conditioned water.

If fish are gasping or hiding and the tests are bad, do a larger water change and ask an experienced keeper for advice.
