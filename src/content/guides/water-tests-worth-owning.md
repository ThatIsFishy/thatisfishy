You can’t see most water problems, so tests are your eyes.

## Start with these four

- **Ammonia:** should be zero in a cycled tank. Any reading means stress for fish.
- **Nitrite:** should also be zero.
- **Nitrate:** will rise between water changes. Many keepers aim to keep it fairly low, so ask what is normal for the species you choose.
- **pH:** tells you how acidic or alkaline the water is. Stability matters more than hitting a perfect number.

## Add these later

**GH** (general hardness) and **KH** (carbonate hardness) matter when you start choosing fish and plants that prefer soft or hard water.

## Which kind of kit?

Liquid test kits are generally considered more accurate than paper strips, and they last for many tests. Buy a kit from a reputable brand, and check the expiry date on the reagents.

## How often

Test every few days while the tank cycles, then weekly. Test any time the fish look unwell or the water looks different.
