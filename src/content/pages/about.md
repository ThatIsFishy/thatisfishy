# **Welcome to That Is Fishy!**

### A little about me and my aquarium journey

**Dear Reader,**

First of all, welcome to my aquarium blog, **That Is Fishy!** You are on the right place if you are looking for fun educative information and a little insight into my personal aquarium journey.

Before I explain why I started this blog, let me introduce myself.

I am Adam, a 27-year-old independent European man currently living in the Netherlands, originally from Hungary. I have always loved nature and animals and I have always wanted to bring them closer to me whether by having them around me or by visiting places where they live. I have been been fortunate enough to travel all around the world and explore the beauty of our planet from the depths of the seas and the heart of jungles to the tops of mountains.

It was never really a question of whether I would study something related to nature at university. I graduated as an Environmental Engineer and later continued my studies in the Netherlands, specialising in Water Technology.

Thanks to this love of nature, combined with my educational background, I was finally able to afford (and convince my dear wife!) to start this hobby, aquarium keeping. I started with a 67 litre (18 US Gallon) aquarium and a second wonderful journey began. (I am not allowed to call it the first since I am happily married). In the future, I will introduce you to this one and my second one and hopefully the upcoming aquariums as well. So stay tuned!

That is enough about me for now. Lets talk a bit about the purpose of this website and the story behind the name, That Is Fishy! I simply found the name funny. I hope you get the joke and the connection behind it and that I am not the only one who finds it amusing.

As you might have gathered I am not an aquarium expert with 30+ years of experience nor do I want to advertise myself as someone who knows everything about this hobby. However, I do have some experience a curious mind and a stubborn streak when it comes to figuring things out. I am always willing to experiment, learn and explore the unknown. And that is exactly where this blog comes in.

For all these reasons, I know how difficult it can be to get started, find relevant and trustworthy information, understanding what is happening inside your aquarium and solve unexpected problems. Sometimes you have to figure things out alone or rely on advice from some guy on an online forum. There were plenty of occasions when my own tank was looking a little... fishy, and I simply could not figure out what was wrong. To be clear, this still happens so do not get me wrong.

Based on these experiences, I decided to start this blog. I want to share my journey, my experiences as a beginner hobbyist and the lessons I learn along the way. Whenever I encounter a problem, I find a solution or discover something interesting, I will do my best to share it here. My hope is that my experiences can help others who are just starting out, as well as those who simply enjoy learning something new.

I want That Is Fishy to become welcoming place where aquarium enthusiasts can find useful information, share experiences and perhaps avoid some of the mistakes I have already made. Furthermore, I want everybody to be able to find a little peace and quiet and truly enjoy the beauty of this hobby. Just grab a chair, place 10 cm from the glass and enjoy having a piece of nature at your home.

**Beauty goes beyond the aquascape. It lies in the fact that, for the animals living there, it represents their entire world. A world for which we are responsible.**
