That Is Fishy is a beginner’s notebook for the aquarium hobby. I’m learning as I go, and I write each lesson down so a newcomer can learn with me.

The name is spelled out as three words: **that is fishy**. It means fish, and it also means “something is off”, which is how a lot of aquarium problems begin.

## What you’ll find here

- [Knowledge Center](/knowledge-center/): short articles on the basics.
- [Fishy Stories](/fishy-stories/): what happens in my own tank.
- [Shops](/shops/): places I have visited or ordered from.

## A note on advice

I’m not a vet or an expert. Treat everything here as one beginner’s notes, and check with experienced keepers before making big decisions about your fish.
