*This is a first version. It will change as I learn.*

## Ground rules

- I’m a beginner. I write about what I’m learning and what I have tried myself, and I say so when I’m not sure.
- I share mistakes as well as successes.
- When I find an error, I correct it and note the correction in the article.
- Please be kind when you write to me.

## Transparency

- When something comes from my own experience, I say so. When it comes from another source, I name it.
- If this site ever earns money from a link, a product or a sponsor, I will say so clearly next to it.
- Photos are my own unless the caption says otherwise.

## How I use AI

I use AI tools to help build this website and, at times, to help me draft, check or tidy up my writing. I decide what gets published, and I check facts against my own experience and other sources. AI makes mistakes, so please tell me if you spot one.

## Not veterinary advice

Nothing on this site is veterinary advice. If your fish are sick, ask an experienced keeper or a vet.
