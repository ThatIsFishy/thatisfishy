## The contact form

When you use the contact form, I receive your message, and your name and email address if you choose to give them. Both are optional. I use them only to read and answer your message.

The form is handled by Netlify, the company that hosts this site. It stores your message so that I can read it. I keep messages only as long as I need them to reply, and then I delete them.

## Your rights

You can ask me to show you, correct or delete anything you have sent me. Use the [contact form](/contact/) to ask.

## Cookies and tracking

This site has no advertising and no tracking. It remembers your light or dark choice in your browser, and nothing else. The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google may see your internet address when a page loads.
